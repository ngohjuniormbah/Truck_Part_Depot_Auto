# Backlink acquisition plan

This is a prospecting and outreach plan, not a claim that links have already been placed. Every link requires the destination publisher, association, supplier or business directory to accept the business. Do not create listings until the legal business identity, service area, website URL, phone, address (if public), inventory and fulfilment claims have been confirmed.

## Earn links by building something useful

The best realistic link asset for this store is not a generic blog. Build one or two technically rigorous resources from verified inventory and OEM data:

1. **Diesel part number and OEM cross-reference lookup** — search by OEM number, manufacturer number, engine family, year and application. Every mapping must be validated; never invent fitment.
2. **Fitment verification guides** — explain which vehicle/engine attributes a buyer must confirm before ordering (year, engine code, emissions configuration, transmission, VIN/ESN where relevant).
3. **Core-return and remanufactured-part explainer** — only if the company really sells remanufactured parts and has a defined core policy.
4. **Part identification photo guides** — original photos with part labels, measurements and verified interchange information.

Then pitch the resource to mechanics, fleet maintenance teams, relevant forums and trade publications because it solves a real problem. Links should point to the exact guide or product/category page that helps the reader, not always the homepage.

## Outreach copy

Subject: Truck-parts reference resource for your commercial vehicle audience

Hello,

I manage Truck Parts Depot Auto, a parts catalogue focused on diesel truck applications including Cummins, Powerstroke and Duramax.

We are preparing a practical reference resource for identifying parts by OEM number and verifying fitment before ordering. It is intended to help technicians and truck owners avoid incorrect-part purchases.

Would your editorial or resource team consider reviewing it as a useful addition to your commercial-vehicle parts resources? I can provide the final resource URL and supporting product/fitment evidence for review.

Thank you,
Truck Parts Depot Auto

Personalise every message. Do not mass-mail the same pitch to hundreds of sites. Only send it once the resource exists and the facts are checked.

## Backlink quality review

For every prospective placement record:
- Exact linking page URL and target URL.
- Whether the page is indexed and relevant to the target market.
- Context and visible link text.
- Link attribute (`follow`, `nofollow`, `sponsored`, `ugc`).
- Whether the listing/article is editorially reviewed or merely paid placement.
- Date requested, date published, owner/contact, and next review date.

Reject private blog networks, bulk directory packages, automated comment links, spun guest posts, irrelevant foreign directories and paid followed links. Google's spam policies explicitly identify paid ranking links, automated link creation, low-quality directories and excessive reciprocal linking as link spam. For legitimate paid placements, the publisher should qualify the link with `rel="sponsored"` (or `nofollow`).

## 14-day off-page sprint

- Day 9: confirm company identity, geographic market, service area and real supplier relationships; check any existing backlink data in Search Console.
- Day 10: contact FinditParts with a real resource suggestion; assess CVSN eligibility.
- Day 11: select supplier dealer programmes that fit actual supply relationships; submit only accurate applications.
- Day 12: pitch one specialist technical resource to relevant trade media and industry communities.
- Day 13: publish the resource on Truck Parts Depot Auto and add internal links from relevant categories/products.
- Day 14: record replies, accepted placements, published URLs and next actions. Do not report a link as acquired until the live linking page has been checked.

## Measurement

Use Search Console's Links report as one source of discovery, but verify important placements manually. Monitor referring domains, referral sessions, organic landing-page conversions and assisted leads. Do not optimise for raw backlink count: relevance, trust, editorial context and qualified referral traffic matter more.
